## Limits of this evaluation

- The labelled set is small (written for this MVP) and was written by the builder, so it measures whether each rule works as specified, not real-world accuracy.
- The rule engine is pattern-based: paraphrases outside its patterns are left to the AI judge, which is why the AI section exists.
- AI results vary between model versions. The model name is recorded on every check row and in the audit log.
